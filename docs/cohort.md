# Initial cohort rationale

The initial bounded corpus uses NVDA, AMD, INTC, AVGO and QCOM because they form a meaningful semiconductor/advanced-computing comparison set with recurring 10-K filings, substantial narrative risk disclosures, structured XBRL facts, temporal depth and cross-company exposure themes such as export controls, supply chain, competition and AI infrastructure.

CIKs are not frozen by hand in configuration; they are resolved from the SEC ticker mapping at runtime.
